Installation

Professional Replacement Window Installation

Replacing a window is different from new construction — the opening already exists, and it is rarely perfectly square after years of settling. Getting the fit, the flashing, and the seal right is what separates a replacement that lasts from one that leaks or fails in the next storm. That is why choosing the right replacement windows contractor matters as much as choosing the new glass.

As a licensed replacement windows company, we remove and replace windows on residential homes across Florida — from single-family houses to condos, from Miami to Jacksonville — and we handle the entire job in-house: measurement, permitting, removal, installation, and final inspection. You get one dedicated project manager as your single point of contact for the whole project.

Every replacement window we install is Category 5 rated impact glass, sourced direct from manufacturers including ES Windows and Tecnoglass. That upgrade blocks up to 99% of UV rays, cuts outside noise by up to 50%, and typically lowers both your energy bills and your homeowner's insurance premium — an upgrade your old windows never gave you.

FAQ

Replacement Windows: Common Questions

Straight answers to what Florida homeowners ask us most.

Replacement windows are built to fit into your existing frame opening, so the surrounding wall, siding, and interior trim do not need to be disturbed. New-construction windows are used when a wall opening is being built or resized, like during a remodel. Nearly all of the work we do is replacement — matching new impact glass into your home's existing openings.

The estimate itself is free. What installation costs after that depends on the number, size, and condition of your existing openings, so any honest number starts with a measurement. With financing, installation works out to as little as $5/month per window with $0 down. The fastest way to get a real figure for your home is a free replacement windows estimate — it is itemised, and there is no obligation to proceed.

In most cases yes, if you upgrade to impact-rated glass. Florida insurers commonly offer wind mitigation discounts for homes with code-approved impact protection on every opening. The exact saving depends on your carrier and policy, so ask your insurer what documentation they need — we can supply the product paperwork from your installation.

Installation on a typical residential home is usually finished within one to a few days once the windows arrive, since we are fitting into openings that already exist. The longer part of the timeline is manufacturing and permitting. Your project manager gives you dated expectations up front and keeps you updated.

Check three things: that the company is a licensed contractor and insured in Florida (ours is CGC1526707), that they pull the permits and see the county inspection through rather than leaving it to you, and that the quote is itemised so you can see exactly what glass and frames you are paying for. Be cautious of any replacement windows company that quotes without measuring your existing openings.
