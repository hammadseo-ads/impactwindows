HOA & Architectural Review

Your Permits and HOA Approval, Managed for You

In most Parkland communities you cannot change a window until the architectural committee approves the design in writing. We manage that paperwork, and the city permit, for you.

01

We measure and specify

Every opening measured on site, then the exact window and door models written down with their frame finish and glass type, so the committee sees what will actually be installed.

02

We put the documentation together

The Florida Product Approval or Miami-Dade Notice of Acceptance sheets, the colour and finish schedule, and the drawings your committee asks for, in the format they want them.

It goes to the committee

Your project manager stays on it with your committee or property manager until there is an answer. Most Parkland committees come back within two to four weeks.

Permit, then install

Once the approval is in writing we pull the permit. In Florida only a licensed general contractor can legally pull a permit for impact work, and our licence is CGC1526707.

Insurance

What This Takes Off Your Premium, Every Year

A discount happens once. A wind mitigation credit comes back every single year you own the house, and it is the reason impact glass pays for itself on a Parkland home.

Wind mitigation credit
$1,000 to $2,800
a year, every year you own the home
Typical yearly wind mitigation credit on a Parkland home once every opening is impact rated. Your carrier sets the final figure.
  • Florida law requires insurers to give a premium credit for impact rated windows and doors, based on a wind mitigation inspection of your home.
  • The credit is at its largest when every opening is protected, not just the front of the house. That is why we quote the whole envelope, not a few windows.
  • We provide the insurance discount documentation, with the product approval numbers that go with it, so your carrier has what it needs to apply the credit.
  • On a South Florida policy the windstorm portion is the biggest slice of the premium, so this is worth raising with your carrier before you sign anything.
